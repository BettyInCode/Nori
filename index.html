— WHO WE ARE

Shaping Futures Through Knowledge & Discovery

Founded in 1962, Nori Secondary School has a rich heritage of academic excellence. We believe in fostering a community where curiosity is celebrated and every student is empowered to reach their full potential.

Our Mission

To provide a rigorous and inclusive education that equips students with the knowledge, skills, and character to succeed in a global society.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution recognized for innovation, academic excellence, and the holistic development of every learner.

Key Milestones

1962

Foundation Laid

Nori Secondary School opens its doors with an initial enrollment of 150 students.

1985

County Recognition

Awarded the County Excellence in Education award for outstanding academic results.

2005

Country Expansion

Established intercounties exchange programs with partner schools across Kenya.
